Ghana Muslim Mission Senior High School

Ghana-Muslim-Mission Senior High School is an institution which was established on the 21st of October 2008 and was named Beposoman SHS. It was later on moved to "Eye Asem" junction and named Ghana Muslim Mission Senior High School.The school offer some programmes like General-Science,General-Art, Home-Science,Business,Visual Art And Arfabic Studies.
